      Why crop?
      
The drop-in fit works from the picture’s edges. Cropped edge-to-edge to
        the board, it lands nearly aligned and every hole stays big enough to read.
        Knobs, jacks and bench left in the frame squash the board small and pull
        the fit off.

      
      
Only the traced copy is cropped. The full picture stays in the reference
        pane, knobs and all, so you can still read what the crop cut away.
